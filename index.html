01

A Website That Actually Works

Not just a pretty page. When someone fills out your form, the lead goes straight to an app on your phone instantly. There's also a live chat widget on the site, so when a visitor types in their name, number, and what they need done, two things happen right away:

  • You get a text alerting you to the new lead
  • The customer gets an automatic text letting them know you got their request and will follow up soon

Nothing sits in an inbox getting ignored. Everything connects to your Google Business page too, so leads flow in from every direction and land in the same place.

02

Missed Call Text Back

You can't always answer the phone when you're on a job. This system covers you. If you miss a call, the caller automatically gets a text like:

"Sorry we missed your call, we're with a client right now. Text us here and we'll get back to you, or you can book an appointment at [your website]."

That keeps the lead instead of losing it to the next business they call.

03

Automated Google Review System

After finishing a job, you tag the customer in the system and they get sent a link. If they leave 4 or 5 stars, it takes them straight to your Google review page. Below that, they're given an option to leave private feedback instead, which keeps you compliant with FTC rules around review gating.

If someone doesn't leave a review right away, the system automatically follows up with them week after week until they do, no manual reminders needed.

04

One Unified App for Everything

All your texts, calls, and contacts, including Facebook and Instagram messages, live in a single inbox on your phone. No more juggling five different apps or losing track of conversations.

05

Year-Long Follow-Up Sequence

Once a job is done, customers get followed up with every 2 to 3 months for the next year, keeping you top of mind for referrals and repeat business. Most small businesses lose customers simply because they're forgotten. This keeps that from happening.

The Bottom Line

This whole system is built to plug the holes where small businesses typically lose leads: missed calls, missed texts, reviews that never get left, and customers who forget you exist after the job is done. It's the kind of automation big companies already use, built and priced for small businesses.

Most Asked Questions

The things people almost always ask once they've seen the walkthrough. If yours isn't here, bring it to the call.

01How much does this cost?

Pricing depends on which pieces you turn on and whether you need a new site built or we're plugging into what you already have. You'll get an exact number on the call, not a range and not a surprise later.

There's no charge for the call itself, and no obligation to move forward after it.

02Am I locked into a long contract?

No. This is built to earn its keep month to month. If it stops making you money, you shouldn't be trapped in it.

We'll go over the exact terms on the call so there's nothing buried in fine print.

03How long before it's actually live?

Most of the automation, missed-call text back, the review system, the unified inbox, can be running within a few days, because it's configured, not built from scratch.

A brand-new website takes longer since it needs your photos, services, and service area. We'll set a real date on the call instead of a vague estimate.

04What do you actually need from me to get started?

Less than most people expect. Roughly:

  • Access to your Google Business profile
  • Your current phone number and who answers it
  • A list of your services and the area you cover
  • Photos of your work, if you have them

We handle the setup, the wiring, and the message writing. You're not being handed a login and left to figure it out.

05Do I have to change my phone number?

No. You keep the number your customers already know and the one printed on your truck and business cards.

The system works alongside it, so calls still ring your phone the way they do now. The difference is that a missed call no longer means a lost customer.

06What if I already have a website I like?

Then we don't rebuild it. We can attach the chat widget, the forms, and the lead routing to the site you already have.

The rebuild is only worth it if your current site isn't converting visitors into calls, and we'll tell you honestly which situation you're in.

07I'm not tech-savvy. Can I actually run this?

If you can use text messages, you can use this. That's genuinely the whole learning curve.

Everything lands in one app on your phone. You reply to a customer the same way you'd reply to a friend, and the automation handles the rest in the background.

08Is the automated review system against Google's rules?

No, and this is a detail a lot of cheaper setups get wrong.

Every customer gets the same request and the same opportunity to leave public feedback. The private-feedback option sits below it as a choice, it isn't used to filter unhappy customers away from your Google listing. That's what keeps it compliant with FTC rules on review gating.

09Who writes the texts and the follow-up messages?

We do, and then you approve them. They're written to sound like your business rather than a robot, because customers can tell the difference immediately.

You can change any message at any time, and if a specific line doesn't sound like you, we'll rewrite it.

10What if I'm already using another CRM or scheduling tool?

Bring it to the call. In a lot of cases this replaces two or three tools you're already paying for, which means the real cost is lower than the sticker price.

Where it makes sense to keep what you have, we'll connect to it instead of forcing a switch.

11If I cancel, do I lose my customer list?

Your contacts and your leads are yours. You can export them, and we'll help you do it rather than making it difficult on the way out.

Your Google reviews aren't going anywhere either, those live on your Google profile permanently, no matter what you do with us.

12How soon will I actually see results?

Missed-call text back and the review requests are the fastest, those tend to show up within the first couple of weeks, because they're catching business you were already losing.

The year-long follow-up sequence is the slow compounding one. It's built for the referral or repeat job nine months from now that would otherwise have gone to whoever the customer happened to remember.
