01

A Website That Actually Works

Not just a pretty page. When someone fills out your form, the lead goes straight to an app on your phone instantly. There's also a live chat widget on the site, so when a visitor types in their name, number, and what they need done, two things happen right away:

  • You get a text alerting you to the new lead
  • The customer gets an automatic text letting them know you got their request and will follow up soon

Nothing sits in an inbox getting ignored. Everything connects to your Google Business page too, so leads flow in from every direction and land in the same place.

02

Missed Call Text Back

You can't always answer the phone when you're on a job. This system covers you. If you miss a call, the caller automatically gets a text like:

"Sorry we missed your call, we're with a client right now. Text us here and we'll get back to you, or you can book an appointment at [your website]."

That keeps the lead instead of losing it to the next business they call.

03

Automated Google Review System

After finishing a job, you tag the customer in the system and they get sent a link. If they leave 4 or 5 stars, it takes them straight to your Google review page. Below that, they're given an option to leave private feedback instead, which keeps you compliant with FTC rules around review gating.

If someone doesn't leave a review right away, the system automatically follows up with them week after week until they do, no manual reminders needed.

04

One Unified App for Everything

All your texts, calls, and contacts, including Facebook and Instagram messages, live in a single inbox on your phone. No more juggling five different apps or losing track of conversations.

05

Year-Long Follow-Up Sequence

Once a job is done, customers get followed up with every 2 to 3 months for the next year, keeping you top of mind for referrals and repeat business. Most small businesses lose customers simply because they're forgotten. This keeps that from happening.

The Bottom Line

This whole system is built to plug the holes where small businesses typically lose leads: missed calls, missed texts, reviews that never get left, and customers who forget you exist after the job is done. It's the kind of automation big companies already use, built and priced for small businesses.
